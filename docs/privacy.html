Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Daily Verse Studio ("the app") is operated by Wellspring Studio LLC ("we") at word.wellspringstudiollc.com. It is a private, password-protected tool. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Information the app processes

  • Verses and videos. The daily verse, generated narration, captions, and videos are stored on our server.
  • AI processing. Narration, word timing, and caption drafting run on our own server. Verse text is not sent to outside AI services.
  • TikTok account data. If TikTok is connected, the app uses your TikTok open ID, display name (through the user.info.basic scope), and access tokens, stored only on our server, to upload the videos you choose.

How information is shared

  • TikTok receives the video you choose when you click "Send to TikTok drafts" (through the video.upload scope), under the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • Daily Discovery Bible Study receives a request for the verse of the day.
  • Stock footage (Mixkit) and open speech models (Hugging Face) are downloaded by the server. No personal data is sent.

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Your choices

Disconnect TikTok in the app or in your TikTok settings at any time. Email contact@wellspringstudiollc.com to have stored videos or tokens deleted.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

Updates will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: contact@wellspringstudiollc.com.
