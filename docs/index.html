Daily Verse Studio · The Word

A moment in the Word, every day.

Daily Verse Studio is a tool by Wellspring Studio LLC that turns the verse of the day into a short vertical video for The Word, a free Bible app. Each video reads the verse aloud with a natural voice, highlights every word as it is spoken, and sets it over calm nature footage and gentle music.

How it works

  1. Today's verse comes from Daily Discovery Bible Study.
  2. One click narrates the verse, times every word, and renders a 1080 × 1920 video in The Word's style.
  3. An AI model drafts a caption, which is reviewed and edited before posting.
  4. Optionally, the video is sent to the creator's TikTok drafts, where they decide whether to post it.

Read Scripture free, with no ads or account, in The Word.
